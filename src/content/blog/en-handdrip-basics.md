Dark roast coffee is loved for its rich body and sweetness. At the same time, it is delicate: over-extraction quickly brings out harsh, bitter notes. Here are the basics for brewing it consistently and deliciously at home.

## 1. Use a medium-coarse grind

Dark roasted beans are brittle and release their compounds easily. Grinding too fine leads to over-extraction, so aim for a **medium-coarse grind** — slightly coarser than granulated sugar.

## 2. Keep water at 88–90°C

Rather than water straight off the boil, use water that has settled to around **88–90°C**. Temperatures that are too high emphasize bitterness and astringency.

## 3. Bloom carefully

First, pour just enough water to wet the grounds and let it **bloom for about 30 seconds**. This releases carbon dioxide from the beans and stabilizes the rest of the extraction.

## Summary

For dark roasts, the basics are "slightly lower temperature, medium-coarse grind, shorter extraction." Try it first with our KURO Blend.
