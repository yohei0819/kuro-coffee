When choosing coffee, you will often see the words "single origin" and "blend." Neither is better than the other — they simply offer different ways to enjoy coffee.

## What is single origin?

Coffee made from beans of a single region or farm. The character of that place stands out, letting you enjoy distinct features such as **fruity acidity or floral aromas**.

At KURO COFFEE, "Morning Light (Ethiopia)" and "Colombia Huila" fall into this category.

## What is a blend?

Coffee that combines beans from multiple origins, designed for balance. Its appeal is a **stable, consistent cup** and the ability to express an intended character.

Our signature "KURO Blend" pursues a balance of deep body and gentle sweetness.

## Tips for choosing

- On days you want to enjoy aroma and individuality, choose **single origin**
- When you want a reliable everyday cup, choose a **blend**

If you can't decide, the best approach is simply to taste them side by side.
